# Post for 2026-07-30

## Post text

An Indian startup just went from launch to unicorn in about a year. $130 million raised, $1.5 billion valuation, 200,000-plus paying customers, all selling one idea: describe your app in English and agents build it for you.

I want to believe that is the hard part solved. It is not.

InfiNotes went profitable in five weeks on zero marketing. Not because the code shipped fast, agents can already do that today. Because we knew exactly which one problem to solve for a student, and what made them open the app again the next day.

Agents will happily build you a beautiful app for the wrong problem, just as fast as the right one. Speed of build was never our real bottleneck. Knowing what to build, and whether anyone sticks around after day one, still is.

I direct AI to build everything I ship. It has never once told me which feature to cut, or why a student stopped coming back. That judgment stays entirely mine to carry.

The tool got faster. The taste it needs still has to come from a human.

If you build with AI right now, what is the one decision you never let it make for you?
