# Post for 2026-07-27

## Post

AI funding in India just had a rough week. Good.

Indian startups raised 209 million dollars in the week ending 25 July, and AI wasn't the story for once. Manufacturing, aerospace, enterprise software and healthtech led. Zetwerk alone pulled in a 52 million dollar pre-IPO round.

Every time this happens, someone posts that AI in India is cooling off. I'd push back.

What's cooling is AI that only existed to raise the next round. A product built to impress a term sheet behaves differently from one built to solve someone's Tuesday afternoon problem.

InfiNotes never had outside capital to depend on. It went from launch to profitable in five weeks, on zero marketing spend, because students kept using it and paying for it. No round to defend, no burn rate to justify, just usage.

That's the real filter this dip applies. Not "is AI over," but "was this product ever standing on its own two feet."

If your AI product needs the next round to survive, the market just told you something. If it doesn't, this week changes nothing for you.

Building AI in India right now: are you optimizing for the pitch deck, or the user who opens the app tomorrow morning?
