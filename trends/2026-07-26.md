## Recommended
ProLearn raises Rs 30 crore pre-seed, led by BEENEXT, to build an AI learning companion for Indian students
https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/prolearn-raises-inr-30-cr-pre-seed-round-led-by-beenext

- Verified facts: ProLearn was founded in April 2026 by Ravneet Singh, a former Vedantu technology leader. It raised Rs 30 crore in a pre-seed round led by BEENEXT, with Eximius Ventures, Antler and angel investors participating. The product is described as a real-time, conversational AI learning companion for K-12 and competitive exam students (JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT), built to listen, explain, ask questions and adapt to pace rather than have students passively watch recorded lectures. The company says the funds go toward product and engineering work ahead of a public launch, meaning it has not launched yet. (https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/prolearn-raises-inr-30-cr-pre-seed-round-led-by-beenext, cross-checked at https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/prolearn-secures-%E2%82%B930-crore-pre-seed-round-led-by-beenext-to-build-personalised-ai-learning-for-indian-students/)
- Why Achal can add a real POV: this is, almost word for word, the category of his own unlaunched AI study companion, arriving pre-launch with institutional money behind it. He has a live comparison to make between a well-capitalised, pre-launch bet on a learning companion and his own path with InfiNotes: ship to real students first, let usage and word of mouth prove the idea, then decide what to build next. He can speak to the specific product bet ProLearn is making (real-time conversational tutoring vs passive video) from the inside, as someone who has actually watched Indian students use an AI study tool day to day.
- Suggested angle in one line: what a newly funded, pre-launch AI study companion gets right and what it still has to prove, seen through the eyes of someone who already shipped one.
